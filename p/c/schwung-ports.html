Schwung ports

Six Ableton Move Schwung modules - HUSH ONE, Junologue Chorus, Midiverb, PSX Verb, TapeDelay and TAPESCAM - compiled unchanged as MPC/Force plugins with MPC screen skins. Midiverb needs your own Alesis ROMs.

Instruments (1)

  • HUSH ONE: Monophonic subtractive bass synth modelled on the SH-101: saw, pulse with PWM and sub, noise, resonant filter with its own envelope, LFO, glide, 11 presets and the original's gate and velocity quirks.

Effects (5)

  • Junologue Chorus: Juno-60 chorus emulation: the two classic BBD modes with mix and brightness, for the 80s stereo shimmer on pads, strings and anything that needs width.
  • Midiverb: Alesis Midiverb, Midifex and Midiverb II emulator, running the units' own programs on a cycle-accurate DASP interpreter at 23.4 kHz. Needs your own EPROM dumps: no ROMs are included - put midiverb.rom, midifex.rom and midiverb2.rom in the plugin's roms folder; a unit without its ROM passes audio through.
  • PSX Verb: PlayStation 1 SPU reverb emulation: model, decay, reverb level, input and mix over the console's own comb and allpass structure, grainy character intact.
  • TapeDelay: Tape delay with flutter, tone shaping, stereo width and tempo divisions. A Space-Echo style design by cyrusasfa, ported to the Schwung platform by charlesvestal.
  • TAPESCAM: Tape saturation and degradation: drive, colour, wobble, hiss and tone with age, tape speed, compression and widening - lo-fi colour for drums, loops and whole mixes.
