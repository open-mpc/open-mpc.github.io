Airwindows Singles

Single Airwindows effects ported one plugin each: reverbs (Galactic, Verbity, kCathedral), delays, EQs and filters, compressors, saturation and console channels, each with its own MPC skin.

  • Airwindows GalacticVibe: The pitch-wobble vibrato stage of the Galactic reverbs on its own.
  • Airwindows Infinity2: Infinite-sustain reverb with filtering, damping and allpass diffusion.
  • Airwindows kAlienSpaceship: Enormous sci-fi space reverb from the kCathedral family.

  • Airwindows kCosmos: Vast cosmic reverb with predelay and early reflections.
  • Airwindows Mackity: Mackie-style console distortion: drive it with the input trim, tame it with the output pad.
  • Airwindows MatrixVerb: Classic algorithmic reverb with filter, damping, modulation and room size.
  • Airwindows PitchDelay: Delay with pitch shifting in the feedback path and a resonant filter.
