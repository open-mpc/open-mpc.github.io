NOXED MPC Plugins

Two instruments and three hardware bridges: acid bass, wavetable synthesis, Elektron Overbridge receive/send, and class-compliant USB audio capture.

Instruments (4)

  • Acidline: Monophonic acid bass synth with accent, slide, a transport-synced 16-step pitch/time sequencer and an optional Open303 sound model. Includes six presets.
  • MPC Overwitch: Receives individual or grouped outputs from an Elektron Overbridge device over USB, with MIDI forwarding and adaptive resampling. Based on Overwitch; requires an Overbridge-capable device.
  • MPC USB Bridge: Receives stereo audio from a class-compliant USB device as an MPC plugin track, with MIDI forwarding and drift-correcting resampling. Requires a compatible external USB audio device.
  • Prism Wave: Eight-voice wavetable synthesizer with two oscillators, unison, sub/noise, envelopes, LFOs, a modulation matrix, chorus, delay and WAV wavetable import. Includes eight presets.

Effects (1)

  • MPC Overwitch Send: Sends MPC track audio to the inputs of an Elektron Overbridge device over the shared Overwitch USB connection, with send and dry levels. Requires an Overbridge-capable device.
