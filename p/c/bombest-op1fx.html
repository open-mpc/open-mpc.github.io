OP Style Collection

11 instruments and 7 effects inspired by OP-1, written from scratch, each with its own MPC skin.

Instruments (11)

  • Cluster: Eight self-feedback sine oscillators that drift apart over a note. OP-1 inspired.
  • DBox: A 24-key synthesized drum kit: two oscillators and noise per hit, 12 voices. OP-1 inspired.
  • Digital: Feedback sine oscillator, a second sine with sync, and a bit-flipping XOR stage. Eight voices. OP-1 inspired.
  • DNA: A 128-bit genome played as a one-bit wave through an anti-aliased reader. Eight voices. OP-1 inspired.
  • DrWave: Wave, blend, motion and fold controls over eight voices. OP-1 inspired.
  • DSynth: Two oscillators with FM, a pitch drop for drum thump and an eight-mode filter. OP-1 inspired.
  • Fm: Four-operator FM synth with eight voices and four screen knobs. OP-1 inspired.
  • Phase: Eight-voice phase-distortion synth, designed from the OP-1 screen.
  • Pulse: Two PWM pulse oscillators per voice, eight voices. OP-1 inspired.
  • String: Plucked string with eight voices and an amp envelope. OP-1 inspired.
  • Voltage: Four-control synth with eight voices and an amp envelope. OP-1 inspired.

Effects (7)

  • Cwo: Stereo delay with a frequency shifter in the feedback loop, plus a MIX knob. OP-1 inspired.
  • Delay: Four-knob delay with a 12-preset bank. OP-1 inspired.
  • Grid: A 2D waveguide mesh you feed audio and read back. OP-1 inspired.
  • Nitro: Filter with a drive stage and a MIX knob, 12 presets. OP-1 inspired.
  • Phone: Telephone-band squash with a 12-preset bank. OP-1 inspired.
  • Punch: Bit-crush style punch for drums and buses, 12 presets, with a Detroit boxer skin.
  • Spring: Spring reverb with a 12-preset bank. OP-1 inspired.
