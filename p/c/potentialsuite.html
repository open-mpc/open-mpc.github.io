PotentialSuite

Ten plugins for sample-based beatmaking that share one look (Print Plate, Night Rack or Colour Block): samplers for breaks and songs, a crate-digging player, and effects for vinyl age, drum punch, tape heat, noise, grit and bleed, with matching FX chain presets across Patina, Punch, Grit and Bleed. Install all or pick single plugins.

Instruments (4)

  • PotentialBreaks: Drum-break slicer: picks a random break from your own break folders (WAV or FLAC), chops it to 8 or 16 slices or by hits across the pads and matches it to the project tempo, by re-pitching or stretching. Shuffle the slices and save the groove as MIDI.
  • PotentialCrates: Record digger: one-tap crates (Drum Breaks, Library, Soundtracks, ...) and ROLL dice dig Discogs and stream a record into your track to sample. A PotentialSuite fork of Crate Digger.
  • PotentialNoise: Calibrated pink noise reference for the pink-noise mixing method: one-tap RMS levels from -24 to -12 dB, mono or stereo, full or 40 Hz-16 kHz range, live RMS and peak reading and a level meter.
  • PotentialWax: Melodic sampler in the spirit of Serato Sample: picks or browses songs from your own folders (MP3, WAV or FLAC), detects tempo and key, sets 16 cue points (phrases, bars or hits) on the pads and matches them to your tempo and key, with time-stretch or vinyl-style pitching. Save the chops as WAVs.

Effects (6)

  • PotentialBleed: Ghost bleed: hears each hit and adds quiet synthetic residue behind it (snare wires, cabinet, piano board, tines and more), so chops sound like a real recording.
  • PotentialGrit: Seven lo-fi effects on pads, one at a time: dust (glue, hiss, wobble), crush, resonant filter, three-band kill EQ, tempo echo, chop gate and ring mod, each with its own settings.
  • PotentialHeat: Soft clipper and bus saturator: push drums, breaks or a whole beat into a warm curve (soft, tape or hard) that rounds the peaks off instead of clipping, with tone, ceiling, level match, mix, a live meter and 12 one-tap presets. Pairs with PotentialPunch.
  • PotentialPatina: Retro-colour insert effect: vinyl and tape noise, wow and flutter, tape/tube/cassette drive, bit and sample-rate crush, a tone tilt and a small room, with presets from subtle dust to old radio.
  • PotentialPunch: Transient shaper: more or less punch at the start of every hit and more or less tail after it, level-independent with no threshold, speed and focus (full/lows/highs), soft clip, live meter and 12 one-tap presets for drums, breaks and more.
  • PotentialWarp: Tempo-locked mangler: 16 patterns of half time, tape stop, stutter, reverse and gates, with swing, triplets, 4-bar patterns and a hold-a-pad mode.
