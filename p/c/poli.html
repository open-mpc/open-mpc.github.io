Poli effects

12 effects for vocals and mixing: de-esser, dynamic EQ, multiband, saturation, chorus, delay, reverb, de-noiser, voice shaper, bus processor, a build-up effect and an effect rack. Gen2 (MPC Live III, One G2, Key G2, XL) only.

Effects (12)

  • Poli-Chorus: Chorus-ensemble with Classic, Ensemble and Vibrato modes, a live LFO display, feedback, width, filter, warmth and 51 presets. Gen2 only.
  • Poli-delay: Stereo delay with offset, ping-pong, tempo sync, filtered and saturated feedback, modulation and 75 presets. Gen2 only.
  • Poli-Ess: De-esser: dynamic sibilance suppressor for vocals, with presets. Gen2 only.
  • Poli-Juicebox: Low-latency voice shaper: pitch and formant shifting, spectral tamer, harmonic shaper, formant filter, air and 50 presets. Gen2 only.
  • Poli-Multiband: 3-band compressor with downward and upward compression per band, and presets. Gen2 only.
  • Poli-q: 8-band dynamic EQ: bells, shelves, 6-48 dB/oct cuts, notch, band pass and tilt, with a live curve, a visualizer, Learn (fits a preset to your voice) and 43 presets. Gen2 only.
  • Poli-Saturator: Saturator with Tube, Tape, Clip and Fold types, Smash compression, Crunch, Soften, three-band drive and 65 presets. Gen2 only.
  • Poli-tea: Master bus processor: saturation, 3-band multiband compression, stereo widening and an adaptive true-peak limiter, with 56 presets. Gen2 only.
  • Poli-verb: Stereo reverb with 14 spaces, tempo-synced pre-delay, reflection probability and 101 presets. Gen2 only.
  • PoliPlug: Effect rack on one insert: up to 16 of your installed effects in series plus a parallel section, each with its own page and presets. Reads MPC.settings and the VST2 Plugins folders to find them, rewrites its own skin, and writes a scan report to MPC Documents. Gen2 only.
  • Poliwow: One-knob build-up effect: turn Wow up for risers, sweeps and tails. Gen2 only.
  • Shhhhh: Learn-the-noise spectral de-noiser: Learn, Threshold, Reduction, 6 nodes, Pass and Diff. Gen2 only.
